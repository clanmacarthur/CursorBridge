## LAYER 0 — BLOCKCHAIN

Not a feature. The ground. Every agreement, every token transfer, every validation result, every NFT lease, every vote, every asset change is written to the chain once and readable by all permissioned parties. Smart contracts enforce agreements automatically. No central authority. No single point of failure. Provenance is permanent.

**What goes on-chain:**
Every agreement the Agreements Engine writes. Every token issuance and transfer. Every NFT usufruct right (land, asset, time-bounded use). Every validation result from the Workbench. Every governance vote. Every impact milestone from non-profit funded projects. Every synergiser connection and its subsequent royalty flows.

**Blockchain roadmap note:**
The platform does not require its own chain immediately. Phase 1 uses existing infrastructure (Polygon, Ethereum L2, or similar) with smart contract templates. Phase 2 considers application-specific chain or rollup as volume justifies. The key architectural decision is that blockchain is load-bearing from day one — not retrofitted.

## LAYER 1 — THE AGREEMENTS ENGINE

The single most important layer above the blockchain. One engine. Every relationship in the system governed here. It does not separate into different tools per use case — it applies different lenses to the same underlying logic.

**What it does:**
Reads parameters from all parties (via knobs, wheel selections, direct input, or historical data). Writes the appropriate agreement dynamically. Stores it on-chain. Enforces it through the token economy. Learns from every novel decision a human makes.

**The eight relationship types it governs:**

**1. Personal Consent**
Wellness data sharing. Family permissions. Carer access. Clinical data agreements. Buddy program connections. Every personal data relationship in the system is a consent agreement the engine writes and the user controls.

**2. Business Contracts**
SLAs. Vendor terms. Module licensing. Revenue share. Joint ventures. IP royalties. The engine writes these dynamically from parameters both parties set — not from templates a lawyer drafted. When parameters change (revenue threshold crossed, milestone hit, term expired), the contract updates automatically.

**3. NFT Land Leases — Fractional Usufruct Rights**
This is not property ownership. It is the right to use and benefit from land or a physical asset without owning it, for a defined period, under defined conditions. A farmer with five acres leases two fractionally to three tenants simultaneously, each with different crop restrictions, water access rights, and seasonal boundaries. A fourth party — an investor collective — funds soil remediation and holds a time-bounded right to a percentage of the yield as their return. All of this is one set of nested agreements, written dynamically, stored as interlocking NFTs, governed by the token economy. The landowner sees a wheel showing who has rights to which portions. The tenants see their lease terms. The investor collective sees their stake and yield share. Same land. Different wheels. One agreements engine.

**4. Investor Pooling**
Individual investors pool into collective investment vehicles for specific projects. The engine governs voting rights, governance rules, profit distribution ratios, exit windows, and dispute resolution — all set by the pooling parties via their own wheel configurations. The synergy protocol finds complementary investors just as it finds complementary businesses.

**5. DAO Governance**
When any collective (co-op, community group, investment pool, developer collective) governs itself through the platform, the voting rights, quorum rules, veto powers, and decision scope are set by the members and written by the engine. On-chain, transparent, community-set.

**6. Synergiser Royalty Agreements**
A new contract type for people whose value is connection. When a synergiser identifies or facilitates a connection between nodes that the algorithm had not yet surfaced, they earn a token royalty on the value that connection generates — for as long as the relationship persists. The agreement is written by the engine at the moment of connection and enforced automatically through token flows.

**7. AI Autonomy Gates**
What the AI can do without human approval. What requires a human decision. What triggers escalation. Not settings in a menu — agreements between the user and their AI agent, written dynamically based on the user's risk profile, their track record in the network, and the stakes of the specific action. As trust builds, autonomy expands. As risk rises, gates tighten.

**8. Onboarding + Sandbox**
The same engine in uncommitted mode. A new user or business builds their wheel and configures their parameters. The engine is already running — already mapping their permissions, their connections, their risk profile — but nothing is committed. When they cross the threshold, sandbox state becomes live state. Onboarding and sandbox are not separate flows — they are one continuous journey through the same engine.

## LAYER 2 — VALIDATION WORKBENCH

Shared by all projects. No project builds its own. Trust is the product.

Validation is mandatory for shared truth and committed actions, not for every private draft action. Literature is authenticated and version-controlled. Audio is transcribed and source-tagged. Video is authenticated and indexed. Human inputs are auditable. AI outputs are provenance-tracked. Every claim that is promoted to shared/live state can be traced to its validated source.

**Validation routing policy (authoritative):**
- **Must pass Validation Workbench:** on-chain writes, shared/public claims, cross-org data exchange, automated high-risk decisions, and onboarding state that is being committed from sandbox to live.
- **Can run pre-validation:** private sandbox exploration, local drafts, personal notes, interface experiments, and temporary generation outputs not yet promoted to shared/live.
- **Promotion gate:** any draft output becomes validation-gated the moment it is promoted to shared/live state or used as a decision/input for committed agreements.

This is what makes the system credible in clinical, legal, institutional, and investment contexts. It is not a compliance tool bolted on — it is the epistemological foundation of the entire platform.

## LAYER 3 — MULTI-TOKEN ECONOMY

Six token classes. All usage-based. All deflationary. All governed by the Agreements Engine and recorded on-chain.

| Token | Domain | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| EnergyToken | Power + utilities | renewable generation, grid contribution, conservation |
| FoodToken | Agriculture + nutrition | production, local sourcing, waste reduction |
| TechToken | Digital + AI | module usage, botlet deployment, code contribution |
| LabourToken | Human work | time, skill, training delivered, mutual aid |
| RestorationToken | Environment | carbon offset, biodiversity, soil health, water |
| ImpactToken | Social outcomes | wellbeing, community cohesion, access improvement |
| SynergyToken | Connections | awarded to synergisers (human and AI) for connections made |

**Cross-sector multiplier:** An investment that benefits multiple token domains simultaneously earns a multiplied recoup rate. A solar panel that powers a farm, a workshop, and a community facility earns EnergyToken, FoodToken, and LabourToken flows simultaneously. The AI calculates the cross-sector benefit and distributes accordingly — making multi-benefit investments structurally more rewarding than single-domain ones.

**Ethical and sustainability scoring:** Every node's token recoup rate is modified by its verified sustainability score, environmental footprint, ethical standards compliance, and community impact. Behaving well pays better. This is not values-washing — it is values made economically load-bearing.

## THE NON-PROFIT PILLAR

Structural. Not an afterthought. Not a CSR layer. A load-bearing part of the architecture.

**The flow:**
2–5% of all for-profit ecosystem profits → Non-profit fund → Democratic community vote → Real-world regenerative projects

**Fundraising feeds the fund from outside:**
Crowdfunding. Impact bonds. Institutional grants. ISU token vehicles (investors hold ISU tokens that grant governance rights and yield share from the fund's portfolio). The autonomous fundraising engine runs continuously — surfacing the fund to relevant investors, grant bodies, and impact funders based on its current project portfolio.

**What the fund backs:**
Regenerative farms and land projects. Community infrastructure (shared facilities, maker spaces, digital access). Open-source research and the Living Directory. Subsidised wellness and buddy programme access for vulnerable populations. Charity direct giving (voted quarterly, on-chain disbursement, transparent tracking).

**The circular feedback:**
Every funded project generates validated impact data. That data flows back into the Validation Workbench, strengthening the platform's credibility. Credibility attracts new investment. New investment grows the fund. The non-profit feeds the for-profit feeds the non-profit. Circular.

**Governance separation:**
The for-profit entity cannot direct the fund. The fund is governed by community vote. Professionals verify impact claims. The blockchain makes all flows transparent and auditable. No capture possible by design.

## THE SHARED MODULE ECONOMY

Physical and digital assets as connectable nodes. The same architecture that connects software modules connects vehicles, buildings, land parcels, workshops, cold chains, dispatch systems, and labour pools.

**The core mechanism:**
Any resource underutilised for part of its existence is a candidate. The platform detects underutilisation, finds complementary needs across the network, and proposes connections — without any individual business having to know the others exist. Every new connection opens new adjacent nodes.

**Physical sharing examples:**

**Vehicle fleets:** Nighttime delivery fleet idle during the day → connects to daytime rental demand → the shared lot becomes a third node → opens: cleaning, maintenance, fuel logistics, cold storage, dispatch coordination, driver welfare. One connection generates six new potential nodes.

**Real estate and buildings:** Corporate premises with idle hours → co-working, event space, community facility, short-term storage, last-mile hub. The corporation does not run these — it opens the node and the network activates it.

**Professional kitchens:** Idle between restaurant service windows → connects to catering companies, pop-up food producers, fermentation projects, community meal programmes. Scheduling, hygiene records, validation, micro-payment — all handled by the platform.

**Workshops and fabrication spaces:** Maker spaces, repair cafés, carpentry collectives, small manufacturers — different equipment, different time windows. Tool inventory mapped, availability windows shared, skill profiles connected. Sharing, training partnerships, and complementary production chains surface automatically.

**Hotels, sightseeing, rental:** Same customer, different points of the same journey. Connected availability windows, cross-referred bookings, unified experience the customer did not have to assemble.

**Driver and labour interchangeability:** When vehicles are shared across fleets, drivers become interchangeable. The dispatch module optimises across companies. The driver does not think about which company they serve at any moment. The platform is the consent-governed coordination layer — not a gig platform.

**NFT fractional land leasing:**
A farmer leases two acres fractionally to three tenants simultaneously, with different crop restrictions, water access, and seasonal terms per tenant. An investor collective funds soil remediation and holds a yield-share right. All governed by nested NFT agreements written by the Agreements Engine. Landowner, tenants, and investors each see a different wheel representing the same land. All wheels are live. All flows are on-chain.

**The new node discovery mechanism:**
The most powerful output is discovering what should exist but does not yet. The platform flags negative space — gaps between nodes — as opportunities. A human synergiser or AI agent proposes, specs, and seeds the new node. The coffee expert gets found. The dispatch company gets founded. The cold storage co-op gets formed. These are not algorithmic accidents — they are the intended output of a system designed to find value in gaps.

**Token flows for shared physical assets:**
Every shared asset generates token flows proportional to its multi-sector benefit. Cross-sector multiplier applies — an asset serving multiple token domains earns multiplied recoup. Ethical standards, environmental footprint, and sustainability scores all modify the recoup rate. Good behaviour is economically reinforced.

## THE ENTRY PORTAL (the living network — not a pitch deck)

**What it is:**
Not a pitch. Not a demo. Not an onboarding flow. The first moment of a participant's permanent connection to the network. The moment they begin building their wheel, they are no longer an audience — they are a node.

**What happens the moment someone interacts:**
1. They contribute a real wheel — saved, shareable, endorsable, connectable from the first interaction
2. They opt into the network — the synergy protocol begins scanning for complementary wheels before they finish building
3. They feed the live system via Convex — real-time updates connect their wheel to all others simultaneously

**The Convex layer:**
Wheels from all participants are live in the same landscape simultaneously. When rings overlap across different participants' wheels, the synergy protocol initiates automatically. No single author drives this — the collective intelligence of every participant evolves the landscape continuously.

**Investor grouping via the Entry Portal:**
Investors are not passive. They are nodes with their own wheels and their own synergy potential. The Entry Portal finds complementary investors — matching values, risk profiles, sector interests, and time horizons — just as it finds complementary businesses. Once pooled, the investor collective has its own governance wheel managed by the Agreements Engine.

## CROSS-PROJECT RULES

1. Blockchain is the foundation. Every project writes its agreements, tokens, and validations to chain — none maintains its own ledger.
2. The Agreements Engine is universal. No project builds its own contracts layer.
3. The Validation Workbench is shared. No project builds its own trust layer. Validation is mandatory at promotion/commit boundaries, not for all private draft actions.
4. Wheels + Knobs is the universal interface. No project invents a different UI language.
5. Every project exposes a self-build mechanism. The complexity scales; the principle is universal.
6. The Non-Profit Pillar is structurally separated. No project directs fund flows.
7. The token economy is shared. Projects use the multi-token system — they do not create custom tokens.
8. The Entry Portal is a deployment of Dashboard Builder on its own content — not a separate project.
9. New nodes are discovered, not planned. Every project should expose its gaps to the synergy protocol.
