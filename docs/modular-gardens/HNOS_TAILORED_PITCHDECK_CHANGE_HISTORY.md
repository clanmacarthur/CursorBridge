## Preservation rule

Before any tailored-pitchdeck amendment:

1. record the current version and destination page;
2. record the source pack used;
3. duplicate or preserve the current page/file before amendment;
4. list the exact wording, label, tab or numbered-page deltas;
5. state what remains unchanged;
6. preserve superseded work as history rather than deleting it;
7. mark the new current or review-candidate version explicitly;
8. leave unresolved terms visible and labelled;
9. record the intended change in Notion before editing and the completed change after verification.
